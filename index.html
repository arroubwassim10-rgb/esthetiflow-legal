Shorts Pipeline

Shorts Pipeline is a private, single-owner tool operated by EsthétiFlow. It prepares original short videos (stories about history and mystery, with AI-generated illustrations and narration) and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channel, EsthétiFlow (@esthetiflow).

It is not a public service: only its owner can use it, with the owner's own Google account.

What it does with Google data

  • Uploads the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube channel and sets their title, description, audience and publication time.
  • Reads the statistics of the owner's own channel (YouTube Analytics, read only) to write a weekly report for the owner.
  • Sends notifications and reports to the owner by e-mail (Gmail, send only; it cannot read mail).
  • Stores the owner's videos before publication in a Drive folder it creates (it cannot see any other file).

Shorts Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No data of other people is collected, sold or shared.
