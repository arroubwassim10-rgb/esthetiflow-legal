Shorts Pipeline

Shorts Pipeline is a private, single-owner tool operated by EsthétiFlow. It prepares original short videos (stories about history and mystery, with AI-generated illustrations and voice-over) and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channel, EsthétiFlow (@esthetiflow).

It is not a public service: only its owner can use it, with the owner's own Google account. Through Google OAuth the tool uploads the owner's videos to the owner's channel, reads that channel's own statistics to write a report for the owner, and sends notifications to the owner by e-mail. No data of other people is collected, sold or shared.
